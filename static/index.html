what veil strips on every hop

  • attribution & UTM query params (utm_*, fbclid, gclid, _gl, …)
  • known tracker & ad domains — Google Analytics, GTM, Clarity, Hotjar, Criteo, Taboola and ~100 more
  • tracking cookies (_ga, _fbp, _mkto_*, demdex, …) while keeping your first-party session
  • referrer leakage — upstream only ever sees the site's own bare origin
  • fingerprint headers: Sec-Ch-Ua*, sec-fetch-*, Origin
  • redirect hops get the same scrubbing — trackers can't re-attach params mid-journey
  • your IP goes out through this proxy, not your machine

what no proxy can do — read before trusting it

  • stop a page's own JavaScript from canvas/WebGL/audio fingerprinting — that script runs in your browser
  • see first-party tracking (a site logging you on its own servers)
  • hide the fact that you browse, or what you browse (TLS metadata + the proxy's egress IP)
  • protect websocket or other non-HTTP traffic
zero dependencies · in-memory only, no logs written · telemetry: /stats · reset counters: /stats/reset
for personal privacy. don't point it at sites you'd break the terms of, and treat "no tracking" as "a lot less tracking".
